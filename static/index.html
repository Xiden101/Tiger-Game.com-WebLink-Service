Tiger Game.Com Web Link Usage

  1. Connect your Tiger game.com Web Link or Tiger game.com Internet Cable to your computer.
    1. Web Link: use a standard DB25 serial-to-USB cable.
    2. Internet Cable: use a null-modem DB25 serial-to-USB cable (a male-to-male gender changer adapter may be required).
  2. Power on the game.com and launch Web Link.
  3. Press Start and wait for "Linking..." to appear on screen.
  4. Select the COM port your cable is connected to, then click Connect.

For more information visit gamecom.dreampipe.net
